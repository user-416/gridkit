GridKit

Daily numbers as a grid.

GridKit is a small iOS app. It shows daily numbers as a GitHub style contribution graph on your home screen and lock screen. Steps, workouts, Strava activity, Toggl hours, or anything you count yourself. I made it for myself. You can use it too.

It's in private beta right now. Email me if you want to try it. Health data stays on your phone (privacy policy).
