Your KPIs, every day, at a glance.

GridKit pulls daily metrics from Stripe, Strava, Apple Health, and your own taps — and shows each one as a contribution-graph heatmap on your iPhone home screen and lock screen. The numbers you actually care about, where you actually look.

Coming to TestFlight Currently in private beta.

Built for the metrics that move you

One screen, many metrics, all stitched into the contribution-graph format you already know from GitHub. No dashboards to log into, no email digests to ignore — your KPIs are right there next to your home-screen icons.

Heatmap, not a graph

Every day is one cell. Darker = more. A glance tells you whether this week beat last week — no axes, no scrubbing.

Lives on your home screen

Small, medium, and large widgets. Lock-screen widgets too. Tap-through to a full-year heatmap and rolling stats.

Multi-source, one shape

Steps from Apple Health, revenue from Stripe, activity minutes from Strava, and your own manual counters — all rendered the same way.

Privacy-first

Health data never leaves your device. Stripe and Strava use read-only OAuth tokens stored in your iOS Keychain. No analytics on you.

Tap to log anything

Sales calls. Cold pitches. Reps in the gym. Cigarettes you didn't smoke. If it's a daily count, GridKit can track it.

Always fresh

Background refresh keeps integrations in sync with their source of truth. Open the app and the data is already there.

Pulls data from where it already lives

No re-entry, no double-bookkeeping. GridKit reads the data you already have.

Privacy

We don't want your data. We want it to work.

Health data stays on your device — full stop. Stripe and Strava use scoped, read-only OAuth tokens we store in the iOS Keychain. Our backend (a tiny Cloudflare Worker) relays OAuth handshakes so secrets never ship in the app — and it doesn't store anything about you. No analytics, no tracking, no sale of data.
