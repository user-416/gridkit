Support

Quick fixes first, then a contact link if those don't help.

Get in touch

Email t.gao@yale.edu — include the iOS version, GridKit version (Settings → About), and a screenshot if anything looks off. Response time is usually within 24 hours on weekdays.

Common fixes

"Connect with Strava" shows a redirect_uri error

This usually means our Strava app configuration is mismatched on our side. Email us with a screenshot of the error and we'll fix it within hours (Strava's developer settings update instantly).

HealthKit permission was denied — how to grant it

iOS won't re-prompt for HealthKit access once you've denied it. To grant access:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app.
  2. Scroll to Privacy & Security → Health → GridKit.
  3. Toggle on Steps and Workouts (or whichever sub-types you want).
  4. Reopen GridKit and pull down on Home to refresh.

Heatmap shows yesterday's data, not today's

GridKit refreshes in the background but iOS controls the schedule and may defer it to save battery. To force a refresh, open the app and pull down on the Home screen (the heatmap area), or open a metric's detail view.

Widget shows "—" or stale data

Open the GridKit app once. Widgets get fresh snapshots whenever the app runs in the foreground. If the widget still doesn't update, long-press the widget → Edit Widget → confirm the metric is still selected.

How do I disconnect an integration?

In the app: Settings → Integrations → tap the integration → Disconnect. You can also revoke access directly:

  • Strava: strava.com/settings/apps
  • Apple Health: Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → GridKit → Turn Off All

How do I delete all my GridKit data?

Disconnect every integration (above), then delete the GridKit app from your home screen. That removes the local SwiftData store, the on-device caches, and the Keychain entries. Our Worker doesn't store anything per-user, so there's nothing else to clean up on our side.

Bug reports & feature requests

Open an issue at our GitHub repository or email t.gao@yale.edu with a clear description and screenshots. Bug reports that include the reproduction steps get fixed first.

Security disclosures

Found a vulnerability? Please report privately to t.gao@yale.edu. Don't open a public GitHub issue. We'll respond within 48 hours, fix the issue, and credit you in the release notes (with your permission).
